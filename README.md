My theme for my dataset will be music from Olivia Rodrigo. Rodrigo is a singer, songwriter, former actress, and a major player in the world of modern pop music. She has released three studio albums as well as several singles and EPs. I have been a fan of hers since before she started releasing music, and I am excited to delve deeper into her songs through this project.

### Musical Scores
There are a plethora of scores for Olivia Rodrigo songs across the internet, such as on MuseScore. However, many of them are arrangements or covers made by fans in order to avoid paywalls for the official scores.
