My theme for my dataset will be music from Olivia Rodrigo. Rodrigo is a singer, songwriter, former actress, and a major figure in the world of modern pop music. She has released three studio albums as well as several singles and EPs. I have been a fan of hers since before she started releasing music independently, and I am excited to delve deeper into her songs through this project. The song I have chosen for this assignment is "begged" from her third album "you seem pretty sad for a girl so in love." 

### Musical Scores
There are a plethora of scores for Olivia Rodrigo songs across the internet, such as on MuseScore. However, many of them are arrangements or covers made by fans in order to avoid paywalls for the official scores. 

### Metadata
Rodrigo has a lot of information on her website oliviarodrigo.com under the "shop all music" tab. There is also plenty of data on her social media platforms, as well as on Spotify, Apple Music, and YouTube. Many streaming apps curate playlists of artists' popular music and of music in a similar genre. These platforms also include names of musicians, producers, writers, and other key members in the creation of the song. 

## Audio Recordings
Official CDs and vinyls are available on Olivia Rodrigo's website. Otherwise, all of her music is available for streaming on Spotify, Apple Music, Amazon Music, and YouTube.

## Challenges
It appears that not many people look for metadata on music because there is no one platform that readily indicates the information it contains. There is no one concentrated place online to find all the metadata surrounding a song. Working with this music, I also discovered that there are many hoops to jump through to avoid breaking copyright laws and regulations.
